## Meal plan rules
- For each itinerary item with `kind === 'meal'`, map to MealKind by label/order: en-route lunch → `en_route_lunch`, dinner →
  `team_dinner`, breakfast → `breakfast`, pre-game → `pregame_meal`, boxed (the depart_return item carries meal_id) → `postgame_boxed`.
  (Tip: have A-02 put the MealKind somewhere you can read, e.g. a small exported map from meal_id → kind; you may adjust A-02 code.)
- Candidates: en-route meals → restaurants in `game.route_stop_city` with `route_stop: true`; others → restaurants in `game.city`.
  Prefer `capacity ≥ headcount`; if none qualify, use all candidates.
- Strategy `cheapest`: min `price_per_person` (ties: fewer dietary gaps, then id). `group_friendly`: among `group_friendly`
  (fallback all) min (dietary gaps, price). `hotel_service`: if hotel has team meal service and the meal is
  team_dinner / breakfast / pregame_meal → vendor = hotel at `team_meal_price_pp`; else group_friendly.
- headcount = travelers. `cost = round2(headcount × price_per_person)`. `over_allowance = price > settings.meal_allowance_per_meal`.
- `dietary_gaps` = unique non-`none` diets of traveling players missing from vendor `dietary_options` (hotel: assume it covers all).
- Fill the itinerary meal item's `location` with `"<vendor name>, <city>"`.

## Package rules (ids `PKG-<gameId>-<A|B|C>`)
| Tier | Label | Hotel | Departure strategy | Meals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Economy | lowest total hotel cost with `rooms_available ≥ rooms_total` | day_before_afternoon | cheapest |
| B | Balanced | best value: min `nightly_rate × (1+tax) + 8 × miles_to_stadium` (VALUE_PER_MILE = $8, record in DECISIONS) among those with capacity | day_before_afternoon | group_friendly |
| C | Game-Ready | closest with `has_team_meal_service` and capacity (fallback: closest with capacity) | day_before_morning | hotel_service |

- Skip hotels in `req.excluded_hotel_ids`. If no hotel has capacity, use the largest one (A-09 will flag insufficient_rooms).
- Bus company = `settings.default_bus_company_id`. `round_trip_miles = 2 × road_miles_one_way`.
- Traveling players: `selectTravelingPlayers(data.roster, req.players)` (for diets).
- Infeasible plan → `feasible: false`, `infeasible_reason` from `planDeparture`; still compute costs for comparison.
- `risk_flags`: call `computeRiskFlags(pkg, ctx)` inside try/catch — on `NotImplementedError` use `[]` (A-09 implements it).
- `caps` and `preference` do not change the A/B/C composition (caps → over_cap flags in A-09, preference → recommendation in A-05).
- Always return 3 packages, in A, B, C order.
