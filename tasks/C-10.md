# C-10 · Athlete packet PDF + share my itinerary
**Track:** C · **Level:** EXT · **Estimate:** 45 min · **Branch:** `track-c`
**Depends on:** C-05, A-11 (the packet route allows athletes for `audience=athlete` with their own id)

> Read `CLAUDE.md`. The athlete packet has no costs and no other athletes' personal details beyond roommate and bus assignment.
> Export as PDF with `expo-print`, share through the iOS share sheet with `expo-sharing`.

## Context
- `api.getPacket({ params:{ id: tripId }, query:{ audience:'athlete', athleteId } })` → `{ title, html, share_url }` (with athlete headers).
- `AthleteTripView.share_url` = the public read-only page `/share/:tripId/:token`.
- Track B may have `lib/staff/packet.ts` (print + share helper). Copy the pattern into `lib/athlete/packet.ts` rather than
  importing across tracks.

## Steps
1. `lib/athlete/packet.ts`: `shareAthletePacket(tripId, athleteId)` → getPacket → `Print.printToFileAsync({ html })` →
   `Sharing.isAvailableAsync()` → `Sharing.shareAsync(uri, { mimeType: 'application/pdf', UTI: 'com.adobe.pdf', dialogTitle: title })`.
2. My Trip: a "Share my itinerary (PDF)" button (loading state) + "Copy/share link" → `Share.share({ message, url: share_url })`.
3. Errors → friendly message (e.g. A-11 not merged yet → "Packet not available yet").

## Files
- create `lib/athlete/packet.ts`; modify `app/(athlete)/my-trip.tsx`
