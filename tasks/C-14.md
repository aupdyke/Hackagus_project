## Write `docs/DEMO.md`

and a time budget. Total ≤ 4:30.
1. **Season over budget** (Staff → Season): "Six away trips, budget gauge, we're over by $X."
2. **Free-text request → review → packages → AI recommendation** (Trips → at Ohio → Free text → paste:
   `Ohio trip: 68 players, 12 coaches, 11 support staff, bring the equipment truck, can't leave before 2 PM Friday` → Parse → point at
   the review/missing fields → Confirm → 3 packages → Get AI recommendation). Say: "Code computes every number; AI only picks an ID and explains."
3. **What-if**: "Leave three players home" → diff shows new totals.
4. **Streamed memo**: Write memo → streams live.
5. **Publish** (Rooming → Generate → Publish step → Approve → Publish).
6. **Athlete**: Switch → pick a traveling athlete (write down a good player id in advance) → My Trip → Assistant: "Who's my roommate?"
   → answer with a citation chip → tap it.
7. **Change notification**: Switch to staff → change the departure (Request: Not before 3 PM) → re-publish → Switch to the athlete →
   Inbox badge → before → after.
8. **Share the PDF** (athlete or staff packet → share sheet).
9. **AI Accuracy screen** (Staff → AI → Accuracy: overall %, per field, fact-guard pass rate; Usage: tokens per feature).
10. **Savings** (Season → Get back under budget → AI pick + explanation → Apply → back under budget).
